How to play

  1. Prop the phone so its back camera sees the whole whiteboard.
  2. Wait for Whiteboard Detected! The topic and blank lines appear on screen, "projected" onto the board.
  3. First, write A–Z once in the small boxes on the side of the board. The game learns how you write each letter (it remembers it next time).
  4. Write one CAPITAL letter on each line with a dry-erase marker.
  5. Step out of the way. After about 2 steady seconds the guess locks in and the tiles turn green, ochre or gray.
  6. Write your next guess on the new row below. You get 6 tries.

Head-to-Head: one phone can watch both halves of the board, or each player can use their own phone with the same game code. If a letter is misread, tap ⌨ and type the word instead.
